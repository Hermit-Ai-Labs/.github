### Hermit is a private AI that lives on your iPhone.

You download it, it downloads a small model once, and from then on every answer comes from the chip in your pocket.
Turn on flight mode and it keeps working, because there is no server to talk to.

- **On the device.** The model runs on your iPhone. Nothing you type leaves it.
- **No account.** No email, no phone number, no profile.
- **No logs.** There is nothing to leak, sell or hand over.
- **Frank answers.** Adult questions get adult answers, without the lecture.

Coming later: a wallet kept in the Secure Enclave, token checks before you buy, swaps that wait for your Face ID, and
agent payments over x402 and Stripe. The AI stays offline; only the crypto goes online.
