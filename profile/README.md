## Hermit AI Labs

**We build a private agent that is loyal to one person: you.**

Hermit thinks on your device, holds your keys on your device, and moves money only when you approve it. The model
runs on the chip in your hand, so your questions, balances and plans never reach a server we run. Turn on flight
mode and it keeps answering.

> **The AI is offline. The money is online.**
> Reasoning stays on the device. Only a transaction you approved, signed on the phone, ever leaves it.

### What Hermit does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Private by construction** | Open-weight models run fully on the device. No account, no conversation store, no telemetry on what you ask. |
| **Earn** | Live DeFi yields across chains, with deposits you sign yourself. |
| **Trade** | Perps on Hyperliquid and cross-chain swaps, from a plain-language request to a signed order. |
| **Pay** | Send to any address or payee, and fund your wallet from any chain or exchange. |
| **You approve everything** | The agent prepares; you decide. Every money move goes through one approval sheet, locked by Face ID. |

### Principles

1. **Local first.** If a feature needs our server to think, it does not ship.
2. **Nothing to hand over.** Data we never collect cannot be leaked, sold or subpoenaed.
3. **Open weights.** Hermit's models are published openly, so anyone can inspect what runs on their phone.
4. **Loyal by design.** The agent works for the person holding the phone, and never moves money on its own.
